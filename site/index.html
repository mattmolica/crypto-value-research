3 · Read with care

Limits of these numbers

  • The forward record is the real test, and it needs months and several saved score runs before it says anything.
  • The full value score can't be rebuilt for past years: holder payouts, supply growth and circulating supply have no free history. The backtest tests P/S only.
  • About 45 tokens a week over about five years, but crypto moves together, so there are only a handful of truly separate periods. On the original 14 tokens, one token (AERO) carried the whole result; the wider set fixes most of that.
  • Survivorship: the backtest uses today's big revenue earners. Tokens that faded never made the list.
  • P/S here uses fully diluted value. About half the tokens don't have a fixed supply, so their past supply is estimated from today's; the exact-supply check above points the same way.
  • Weekly starts with 26 or 52 week holds overlap heavily. The tables show how many truly separate periods there are.
